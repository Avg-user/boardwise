- **Employment Standards** (pay, hours, notice, termination pay, leaves): 1-877-427-3731 (Edmonton 780-427-3731), https://www.alberta.ca/contact-employment-standards. Complaints: https://www.alberta.ca/file-employment-standards-complaint. It handles notice and termination pay; it doesn't decide whether a firing was unfair. Wrongful dismissal is a court claim, so see a lawyer.
- **Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)** (unsafe work, chemicals, missing safety data sheets, and complaints about being punished or fired for raising safety concerns): OHS Contact Centre 1-866-415-8690 (Edmonton 780-415-8690). Report a concern online, anonymously if you want: https://www.alberta.ca/file-complaint-online. Call right away if someone is in immediate danger. This is also where to complain if you were fired, disciplined or threatened for raising safety concerns: within 180 days (OHS Act s. 19).
- **WCB-Alberta** (Workers' Compensation Board: work injuries and work-related illness, including from exposures): 1-866-922-9221, https://www.wcb.ab.ca
- **Alberta Human Rights Commission** (only discrimination or harassment based on protected grounds such as race, gender, disability, age or religion; not safety complaints, including being fired or threatened over safety concerns): 780-427-7661 (Edmonton), 403-297-6571 (Calgary), toll-free by dialling 310-0000 first. https://albertahumanrights.ab.ca
- **Finding a lawyer**: Boardwise's law firm directory, or the Law Society of Alberta's Lawyer Directory at https://www.lawsociety.ab.ca
- A union representative, if the user belongs to a union.
